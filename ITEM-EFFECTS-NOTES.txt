Item sound / cosmetic / wearer-status options (Mindustry v160.7)
=============================================================

Edit config/alphystuff/shop.hjson. Config save/load preserves these options.
Existing item definitions remain valid. The original 45 shop items remain; three
additional temporary Workbench items demonstrate the new options. Delete or replace
these definitions when remaking the catalog; existing configs are not auto-reseeded.

Subweapon firing audio
---------------------
Any SUBWEAPON may specify:
  subweaponShootSound: "shoot"
  subweaponSoundVolume: 0.6
  subweaponSoundPitch: 1

The sound is a field name from mindustry.gen.Sounds, not a filename. "none" or an
unknown sound is silent. Volume is clamped to 0..2; pitch to 0.25..3 on config load.
The server emits native positional soundAt packets at the firing barrel, only when
that barrel fires a real bullet. Mirrored barrels each have their own firing sound.
The bundled/default subweapons now use "shoot". No audio assets enter the plugin.

VFX-only item type
------------------
  attachMode: VFX
  vfxStatus: "burning"
  haloEffect: "none"
  haloInterval: 18
  haloRadius: 0

VFX items emit particles on the unit, without an attachment sprite, rope or geometric
HaloPart. They do not apply gameplay statuses by themselves. vfxStatus borrows the
named status's Effect, not its damage/buffs/debuffs. Alternatively, haloEffect names
a mindustry.content.Fx field directly (e.g. "heal") and takes precedence. haloInterval
is in ticks (60 ticks/second). Existing halo colour/anchor options also work.
Only active slots 1-4 emit these cosmetic particles; VFX items in slots 5-10 have no
world sprite, no particles and no status grants, but retain the normal carry penalty.

Real wearer statuses (works with ANY attachMode)
-----------------------------------------------
  wearerStatuses: ["overclock"]

Only active slots 1-4 grant these statuses. Duplicate grants are combined per wearer.
The grant is refreshed while equipped. Moving the item out of the active slots,
removing it, changing units, or destroying the rig removes statuses granted by the
item, including permanent statuses such as overdrive. Pre-existing statuses are not
claimed or removed. A later external extension beyond the item refresh duration is
also left in place. Mindustry does not track per-source status ownership; simultaneous
same-status applications still obey its normal global status and reaction rules.
Immunities and normal status interactions are respected. none/dynamic, unknown names
and reactive one-shot damage statuses are not accepted as continuous wearer grants.
These are real native statuses; their native particles may appear in addition to a
configured cosmetic emitter. Use only vfxStatus for purely cosmetic effects.
